About Krish App

Krish App – Sarkari Services is a mobile-first citizen information platform that brings together links to official government websites, schemes, certificates, jobs, education, health, banking, transport, pensions and public services. It does not replace any government department; each service opens its respective official website. Always verify important information on the official portal before submitting an application or payment.

Designed for mobile users: compact cards, quick search, direct links, PWA support and easy navigation. The app is intended to make commonly used government portals easier to find from one place. Krish App is an independent citizen-help directory, not a government department. Government applications, login, tracking and payments are completed on the respective official portals. Share the app with family and friends so they can reach useful services faster.
